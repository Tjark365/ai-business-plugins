# B2B Lead Finder

## Inputs
Collect or infer: offer, ICP, geography, industry, company size/revenue, exclusions, and lead count.

## Free mode
Return at most 3 preview leads with company, location, fit rationale, score, confidence and source.

## Pro mode
Return the full ranked research workflow:
- top 3 first
- company and location
- ICP fit rationale
- current buying signal
- source
- likely buyer role
- estimated opportunity value
- score 0-100
- confidence
- next action
- verified facts vs inference

## Research rules
Use current public sources. Prefer company websites, official registries, filings, investor relations, reputable business databases, and recent trade/news sources. Every material current claim needs a source.

Never invent contacts, revenue, buying intent, or private personal data.

## Score
ICP fit 30%, buying signal 25%, ability to pay 15%, problem/offer fit 15%, timing 10%, evidence 5%.

If Pro entitlement is not supplied by the host, do not bypass it; provide the Free preview.
